Corrections to a name, link or credit, claims of a page, and removal requests are accepted. Contact: to be
announced.

## What is shown

A name as spelled by its creator, the links made public by the creator (profile, tip page, social pages),
the packs listed under that name, and a copy of the public profile picture. The origin of every field is
recorded in the creator's file.

## Corrections

A name, link or credit is corrected on request, given a link that shows the right answer. Each change is
recorded in public history.

## Claiming a page

After verification (a message from the creator's own forum account, or a post on the creator's own page), a
public GitHub username is recorded on the page. Changes to the page from that account are then accepted
without further review. No contact details are recorded.

## Removal

A removal request, for a single pack or a whole page, results in the pack being marked withdrawn and no
longer served. That a removal happened is recorded; the details of the request are not.

## Credits under review

Many credits were inherited as a sentence fragment instead of a name, and some name the wrong person. A
credit that is not certain is flagged for review and marked "Credit being checked" in the list.
